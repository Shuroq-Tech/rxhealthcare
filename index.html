About Us

Comprehensive Care for Your Family

RX Healthcare And Dental Lounge brings general healthcare and dental care together under one roof in Chintal, Hyderabad. Our approach is patient-focused and family-oriented — whether you need a routine consultation or dental care for a child, our team takes the time to listen and explain.
